How it works

  1. Tell us your budget

    Money for groceries, how many days, how many people. Just taps.

  2. Get your shopping list

    Low-cost foods that fill you up, priced for your area.

  3. Cook a week of meals

    Simple recipes from your list. Swap any meal you don’t like.

  4. Find free food nearby

    Food pantries, and benefits like SNAP and WIC, close to your ZIP code.

Why it matters

Less to figure out when money is tight

When every dollar counts, every choice takes work: what to buy, what it makes, whether it lasts the week. Make It Stretch does the math, so people can get to dinner.

  • No account, no names, no ads
  • Works on any phone, in English, Spanish, Vietnamese and Chinese
  • Cities get aggregated reports that never identify anyone
